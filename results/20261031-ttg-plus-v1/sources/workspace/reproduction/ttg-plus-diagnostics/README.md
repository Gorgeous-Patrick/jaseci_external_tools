`overlay.transform` makes one disclosed pre-send insertion in a NEW copied
DIRECT driver, plus an import. It never edits live85026d or primary sources.
`capture.before_send` only selects the declared config/request/generation/role,
fingerprint and occurrence. It decodes the actual P/B/D/E/S packet, preserves
the complete packet and text Bind bytes, and binds native named-parameter
lineage. It rejects unsupported formats. No token, credential or unselected
registration/login Bind is intentionally captured.

The hook records same-connection pre-statement anchor/index/settings/snapshot
census through disclosed diagnostic SELECTs. A recursion guard prevents the
census from recursively capturing itself. The census can warm buffers: these
diagnostics never claim cold-cache behavior or primary-equivalent timings.
The original selected packet is then sent unchanged by the existing driver.
Do not attach this hook to a mutating or source-unreviewed statement.

`explain.inline` runs before the selected original send in that same connection
and transaction. It reuses the original parameters and verifies their encoded
bytes against the actual Bind packet. It performs two bounded EXPLAIN executions,
retaining planning/execution times, all plan nodes/rows/loops/buffers and full
JSON. The original transaction/snapshot/statistics are not cloned or replaced.
A local statement timeout is restored afterward. Parent/child buffer counters
must not be summed. Captures/plans remain provisional until the unchanged full
native workflow, full-response/state/restart oracles and selected actual-query
completion pass. Census/EXPLAIN may warm buffers; timings are diagnostic only.
